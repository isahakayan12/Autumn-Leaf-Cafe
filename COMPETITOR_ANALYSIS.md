## 🎯 Target Customer Personas & Market Insights

1. **Highway Travellers & Airport Passengers (ORR Exit 14 & RGIA Shamshabad)**
   * *Pain Point*: Need a serene, high-quality dining stop away from noisy highway food courts.
   * *Solution*: Prominent highway banner emphasizing 2-minute distance from Exit 14 and 15-minute drive to RGIA Airport, with fast menu preview and instant table booking.

2. **Pet Owners & Outdoor Enthusiasts**
   * *Pain Point*: Limited pet-friendly cafes with spacious outdoor lawns in South Hyderabad.
   * *Solution*: Highlight dedicated pet-friendly garden dining areas with clear visual badges and photo gallery accents.

3. **Weekend Brunchers & Families**
   * *Pain Point*: Long wait times during peak weekend brunch hours.
   * *Solution*: Streamlined WhatsApp reservation widget allowing customers to reserve seating options (Lawn Garden vs. Indoor Roastery) in advance.

4. **Coffee Connoisseurs & Remote Professionals**
   * *Pain Point*: Hard to find specialty artisanal coffee menu details and dietary option breakdowns online.
   * *Solution*: Filterable menu with dish search, category tabs (Breakfast, Coffee, Mains, Desserts, Refreshers), and dietary filters.

## 🚀 Core Essential Feature List & Prioritization

Based on market analysis, the following feature matrix has been established for implementation:

### 1. Direct Instant WhatsApp Table Reservation Engine (Priority: MUST HAVE)
- Instant modal interface for table bookings.
- Captures customer name, party size, date, time, and seating preference (Garden Lawn vs. Indoor Dining).
- Auto-generates a formatted WhatsApp message sent directly to café management.

### 2. Interactive & Filterable Menu (Priority: MUST HAVE)
- Category switching: All, Artisanal Coffee, Breakfast & Brunch, Gourmet Mains, Refreshers & Beverages, Decadent Desserts.
- Real-time text search for dishes and ingredients.
- Dietary filter tags: Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free.
- Highlighting signature dishes with "Chef's Bestseller" tags.

### 3. Ambience & Lawn Photo Gallery (Priority: MUST HAVE)
- High-resolution visual showcase of garden lawns, rustic interiors, fairy lights, and coffee brewing.
- Interactive Lightbox popup modal for viewing full-screen images.

### 4. Dynamic Live Opening Hours Status (Priority: MUST HAVE)
- Automatic calculation of current status (Open Now vs. Closed).
- Displays operational hours (8:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily) with clear visual indicators.

### 5. ORR Exit 14 & RGIA Airport Layover Banner (Priority: MUST HAVE)
- Quick-glance location highlight targeting highway drivers on Outer Ring Road Exit 14 and Shamshabad Airport travelers.

### 6. Customer Testimonials & Reviews (Priority: SHOULD HAVE)
- Google rating highlight (4.9 ★) with verified guest reviews.

### 7. Responsive Navigation & Custom Aesthetic Theme (Priority: MUST HAVE)
- Forest Green (`#1b3323`), Warm Amber (`#d4a359`), and Linen Cream (`#fdfbf7`) color system.
- Mobile overlay navigation drawer and sticky navbar header.
